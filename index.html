History

Estimates [likely, ±20–30% per piece]: the fish is weighed by assumption, not measured, and restaurants vary. Fish and shellfish numbers come from USDA FoodData Central, or from Japan's Standard Tables of Food Composition (8th edition) for items USDA doesn't cover (toro, albacore, uni, ikura, unagi, tamago and others). Tuna, salmon, mackerel, shrimp, snapper, hamachi and the sushi rice use the same values as your macro library's October 17 nigiri estimate. Tap an item's name to see its parts, grams and source.

Portions: nigiri assume 15 g of fish and 22 g of rice per piece unless the item says otherwise; sashimi assume 18 g slices for tuna, salmon and yellowtail. Tokyo counters often use 10–15 g of rice and many US restaurants 25–30 g, so set Rice per nigiri to match the place. Each logged item keeps the numbers it had when you logged it.

Your log lives only on this phone, in this app; nothing is sent anywhere. Use Back up in History to save a copy to your Downloads. App version .
